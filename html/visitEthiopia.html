Cultural Trip to the Omo Valley Tribes of Ethiopia

Experience a 7-day in-depth cultural journey through Addis Ababa and the Omo Valley, discovering local traditions, tribal communities, breathtaking scenery, and authentic Ethiopian hospitality.

Explore ancient rock-hewn churches, majestic castles, royal palaces, and iconic historic sites across northern Ethiopia with expert guidance and comfortable travel arrangements.

Tour to Harar & Dire Dawa

Discover the old walled city of Harar, vibrant markets, rich Islamic heritage, and the lively atmosphere of Dire Dawa on a memorable cultural tour.

Special Omo Valley Tour

Journey across Ethiopia's most treasured heritage sites, including Aksum's stelae and Lalibela's rock-hewn churches, bundled with comfortable lodging, transport, and guided experiences.
